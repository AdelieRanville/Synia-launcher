Synia launcher

Pick an item and the Wikidata page that defines your aspect, then open the result in Synia.

    Leave this empty if the aspect shows queries that are not about one specific item.

    Paste the address of the page. Pages you used before appear as suggestions.

    What is an aspect page? It is a page on Wikidata that tells Synia what to display: a title and one or more SPARQL queries. Synia reads it and shows the results, for an item or on its own.

    You can find existing aspect pages on Wikidata:Synia. To make your own, create a new subpage of Wikidata:Synia on Wikidata (for example Wikidata:Synia:my-aspect), then paste its address here.
