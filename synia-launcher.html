Synia launcher

Choose an aspect page, add the items it needs, and open the result in Synia.

Paste the address of the page, pick a recent one from the suggestions, or browse the list.

What is an aspect page? It is a page on Wikidata that tells Synia what to display: a title and one or more SPARQL queries. You can find existing aspect pages with the “Browse aspect pages” button or on Wikidata:Synia.

Create your own. Create a new subpage of Wikidata:Synia on Wikidata, for example Wikidata:Synia:myaspect, then paste its address here. The name decides how many items the launcher asks for (names are case-sensitive):

  • no dash, such as venue: one item;
  • two names joined by a dash, such as language-language: two items;
  • a name ending in -index, such as library-index: no item.
